## Meaning and scheduling

| Disposition | Meaning | Active-plan treatment |
|---|---|---|
| DEFERRED | A supported optional obligation remains outside current work. | Remove executable tasks; retain the rule and explicit re-entry condition. Do not delete the feature or claim conformance. |
| UNMEASURED | A fact needed to justify a specific implementation is missing. | Record missing evidence; no guessed parsing or repeated investigation without new evidence. |
| WITHDRAWN / NOT ADOPTED | The owner cancelled a proposal, or evidence did not justify adopting it. | Remove implementation/research/gate prerequisites. A withdrawn proposal needs a new owner decision; an unadopted restriction needs new evidence and shared disposition. |
| HOST-SPECIFIC / NO PORT | Another host's internal mechanism is not a common implementation requirement. | Remove cross-host copying tasks; retain common input/result obligations and host-owned maintenance. |
| BOUNDED METHOD | A development method has an agreed stopping condition. | Remove open-ended repetition; retain functional verification and correction of demonstrated defects. |

## Removal work and evidence

Each host removes obsolete active tasks and prerequisites from its own plan.
During the owning unit and final reconciliation, inspect current active guidance,
hooks and relevant code/tests for remnants of withdrawn proposals or unnecessary
cross-host requirements. Remove only confirmed remnants and references that still
prescribe them. Update affected tests for a justified behavioral deletion. Record
absent remnants as absent rather than inventing deletion work.

Historical observations, functioning optional features, common contracts and old
failed-review receipts are not deletion targets. Preserve history labelled as
history. An old checkbox or OPEN cell cannot reactivate completed/withdrawn work.
Deferred items retain a re-entry condition, not an executable checklist. Retire
the temporary task hook/startup routing only at whole-task completion.
